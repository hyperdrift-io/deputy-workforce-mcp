# Deputy Workforce Operations MCP

## Mission

Help managers of shift-based teams understand coverage, workload, timesheet, availability, and
labour signals through the AI assistant they already use, with every result grounded in Deputy
records.

## Product role

This is MCP Maker delivery 001 and its flagship public proof.

## Approved experience pillars

- Read-only by default and by implementation.
- Five useful workforce questions, not an endpoint mirror.
- Evidence and thresholds in every result.
- Local ownership of Deputy credentials.
- Local stdio and secured remote streamable-HTTP transports.

## Rejected directions

- Shift, timesheet, leave, payroll, or employee mutations in V1.
- Employee scoring or performance judgment.
- Multi-tenant hosted SaaS before paid delivery evidence.
- Generic OpenAPI-to-MCP generation.

## Open decisions

- Which Deputy customer or partner supplies the first real workflow validation.
